Hello, world

A small marker in the ground: this is where the notes will live.

I wanted a place that feels quieter than a feed and less formal than a portfolio. A blog is still good at that. It gives half-formed thoughts enough room to become useful without asking them to become announcements.

This site will collect notes on AI, knowledge work, consulting, internet culture, and the little private theatres people build around ambition. Some posts may become essays. Some may stay closer to field notes. The useful distinction is whether they help me think better.

For now, the archive begins here.
